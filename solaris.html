Solaris A/V Engine

The problem

QC of long videos eats hours of skilled work

Reviewing hours-long recordings for technical defects is slow, repetitive and error-prone. Teams either skip it (and ship embarrassing glitches) or burn analyst time watching footage at real speed.

The solution — v2 rebuild

What changed from v1 to v2

  • ≈6.5× faster analysis. Restructured pipeline with code-splitting and smarter row filtering cut wall-clock time per file dramatically.
  • Offline PWA. Installable, works without a connection after first load — field-proof for studio and travel use.
  • Exportable QC reports. One click produces a structured report reviewers can archive or share.
  • Presets & shortcuts. Repeatable configurations and keyboard shortcuts for power users.
  • A/B comparison. Side-by-side runs to validate settings before committing to a full batch.
  • Local-first Pro licensing. License validation runs locally — no phone-home, no vendor lock-in, privacy-friendly.
  • i18n EN/PT-BR + accessibility pass. Bilingual UI with focus-visible navigation, reduced-motion support and screen-reader semantics.

Deployment options
